Eligibility notes

    FAQ

    Why do I enter STCs manually?

    Heat pump hot water STCs depend on the exact model, postcode, installation date and CER register values. Use the REC Registry solar water heater calculator or CER register for the certificate count.

    Does every state have a heat pump hot water rebate?

    No. Some jurisdictions publish direct rebates, some use quote-based certificate schemes, some offer loans, and some do not publish a current household rebate.

    Can state discounts stack with STCs?

    Often yes, but the order matters. For example, Solar Victoria says its rebate is calculated after other discounts such as STCs and Victorian Energy Efficiency Certificates.

    Estimate only. The REC Registry calculator, CER register, product eligibility lists and state program rules determine final eligibility and quoted discounts for a specific installation.
