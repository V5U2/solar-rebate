Eligibility notes

    FAQ

    What is an STC?

    A Small-scale Technology Certificate is the certificate used to calculate the federal battery discount. This calculator estimates battery STCs from usable battery capacity, the taper bands, and the date-based STC factor.

    What is a VPP?

    A Virtual Power Plant connects household batteries so stored energy can be coordinated to support the electricity grid. Some state incentives, including WA and the optional NSW VPP estimate, depend on joining or connecting to a VPP.

    What is usable battery capacity?

    Usable capacity is the amount of stored energy you can actually use. It drives the federal STC estimate and is capped at the first 50 kWh for STC calculations.

    What is nominal battery capacity?

    Nominal capacity is the manufacturer-rated battery capacity. It mainly matters for eligibility because the federal program requires batteries to be within the 5-100 kWh nominal capacity range.

    What is the STC value?

    The STC value is the dollar amount used to turn estimated STCs into a dollar estimate. The calculator defaults to the official STC Clearing House value of $40, but retailer or market values may differ.

    Why do state rebates vary?

    State and territory programs have separate rules. WA has a calculable battery rebate, NSW has an optional VPP incentive estimate, and some other programs are closed or loan-based rather than direct rebates.
